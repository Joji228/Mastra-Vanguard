# Player guide

[← Back to Mastra Vanguard](../README.md)

## Your first mission

Choose **Stage 1**. Move with `A` / `D`, tap `F` to fly, and hold the left mouse button while aiming. Clear the hostiles, then follow the arena marker to the boss. Mission results show score, best score, time, enemies defeated, damage taken, and remaining health.

Gameplay keys follow physical key positions, independently of your typing layout. The full controls are listed in the [README](../README.md#quick-start) and in-game menus.

## Power tips

- **Power Launch:** hold `Shift + W` on the ground for **0.6 seconds** to launch straight upward. Release either key during the charge to cancel.
- **Mach dive:** hold `Shift + S` to reach Sonic Boom in about **0.5 seconds** from a hover when energy and cooldown permit. Hit a deck at Mach speed for a damaging shockwave.
- **Super Beam:** hold and release `Space`. A full charge deals more damage; charging temporarily pauses the click beam.
- **Prism Nova:** press `V` for an area attack. Start with **three charges**; defeating **three enemies** restores one charge.
- **Prism Guard:** press `Q` for a **one-second shield** that costs speed energy and has a five-second cooldown. Block in its first **0.2 seconds** to recover flight energy.
- **Recovery:** health regenerates after **two seconds** without damage. Flight energy recovers while not flying and receives a boost after kills.

## God Mode

Toggle it in the main or pause menu for invincibility, unlimited energy and Nova charges, and increased damage. It works in every stage and the Training Range. Assisted campaign runs have separate best scores.

## Optional stage bonuses

Each awards **+300 score** and can be skipped without blocking the boss.

- **Stage 1:** use Prism Guard near the evacuation relay to protect it.
- **Stage 2:** destroy the crystal node with a beam or Nova.
- **Stage 3:** destroy the reactor regulator to stop its telegraphed vents.

## Training Range

Explore an **18,000 × 9,000-unit sandbox**, separate from the campaign. Spawn any of the seven enemy types or preview any of the three bosses. Use a durable Practice Target, total-damage and rolling two-second DPS readouts, hitbox overlays, and power refills. Clear targets or reset the range at any time.

Ground targets need a nearby deck; use flying targets in open space. Spawned targets are capped at 48. Sandbox score and campaign progression are never saved.

## Settings & accessibility

Open **Settings** from the main menu or pause with `Esc`. Adjust music and SFX volumes, shake strength, **AUTO / HIGH / LOW** effects quality, reduced motion, and reduced flashing. Preferences are saved locally; system reduced-motion preferences provide the initial defaults.

The HUD adapts to smaller browser windows. Control hints can be hidden, and Training Range tools can be minimized. Stage selection supports arrow keys, Home/End, and Enter; Tab reaches the other menu controls. Failed artwork can be retried without restarting a mission.

Use the pause menu to restart an active mission, or press `R` after defeat or completion.
